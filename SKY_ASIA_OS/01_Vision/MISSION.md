# SKY ASIA OS — Mission

**Status:** GOR_GOR_REVIEW

## Positioning

**Asian Culture & Beauty Discovery Platform**

This is **not** a “beauty magazine only.” It is a discovery platform for culture, creators, beauty, brands, festivals, and the next generation across Asia.

## Mission lines

- Discover Asia.
- Support Creators.
- Celebrate Culture.
- Inspire The Next Generation.

## Scope note

Beauty is one discovery lane — alongside culture stories, artist discovery, brand discovery, festivals, and community challenges. Season themes drive what we explore; the platform identity stays discovery-first.
