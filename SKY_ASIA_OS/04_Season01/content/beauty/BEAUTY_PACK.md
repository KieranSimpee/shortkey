## Sticker Inspired Beauty

**Lede:** Makeup that thinks like a sticker sheet — graphic lids, colour blocks, character motifs, and reference boards that treat paper culture as beauty research.

Sticker-inspired beauty is not costume cosplay by default. It is a method: pick a palette from a flake sheet, translate a washi stripe into liner geometry, borrow a character’s blush logic, or build a look that feels like a decorated diary page — layered, playful, intentionally unfinished at the edges. MUAs already use sticker boards as mood references; Season 01 simply names the cultural source.

**Angle for publish:** one tutorial frame + one culture bridge (JP washi restraint vs KR dakku density).

## K-Beauty Trends (sticker-adjacent)

**Lede:** K-Beauty’s playful graphic era rhymes with dakku energy — bright accents, cute packaging language, and fandom-adjacent self-expression.

Look for: glossy lids that read like kiss-cut shine, cheek placement that feels sticker-stamped, and packaging/character collaborations that echo Kakao/LINE sticker-to-IP logic. Polco culture also feeds beauty photography — decorated photo cards as soft-beauty objects.

**Keep honest:** not every K-Beauty launch is “sticker inspired.” Season 01 selects the graphic / character / craft-adjacent slice only.

## J-Beauty Trends (sticker-adjacent)

**Lede:** J-Beauty often meets sticker culture through restraint — clean canvases, soft colour fields, and stationery-adjacent packaging aesthetics.

Washi and techo culture suggest beauty looks with breathing room: muted wash, precise small accents, seasonal motif thinking. Artist-collaboration stationery (Cozyca world) offers a parallel for beauty storytelling — illustrator → everyday object — without forcing product plugs.

**Season 01 note:** beauty slots stay short until Kura taste QC + Simpee partnership path; no invented brand deals.
