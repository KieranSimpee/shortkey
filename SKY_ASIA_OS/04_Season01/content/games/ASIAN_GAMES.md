## Sticker Book Memories

Remember the first sticker book that felt like a treasure chest? Pages waiting. Sheets shining. The impossible decision of whether to use the rarest sticker now or save it forever (and then saving it forever). Across Asia, sticker books were early collecting schools — teaching taste, restraint, generosity (sharing a duplicate), and the soft drama of “almost complete.”

Season 01 invites readers to reopen that memory without needing the original book. What would your childhood sheet look like today — city motifs, character flakes, washi fragments?

## Paper Airplanes

Before planners, there were folded planes launched down corridors and out of windows that should not have been opened. Paper airplanes are sticker culture’s restless cousin: paper as play, not archive. Fold. Decorate the wing with a scrap sticker. Race. Lose the plane to a tree and laugh anyway.

Mini prompt: decorate one wing only. Fly. Photograph the landing. Human post only if you want — no auto-share from ShortKey.

## Four Corners Game

Schoolyard choreography: four corners, one caller, bodies scrambling for safety. Stickers can remake the game for desks — four motif corners on a page (cat / tram / flower / star), a spun pencil as caller, and the “out” corner becomes a sticker you must place.

It is silly. It is social. It is Season 01 saying culture can still be play.
