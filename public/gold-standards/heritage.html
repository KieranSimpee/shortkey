Japanese Komenuka Heritage

Komenuka — rice bran — sits at the quiet center of Japanese beauty ritual: a byproduct of polishing rice that became a soft, everyday polish for skin. This page follows Gold Standard heritage layout: editorial left, timeline right.

Documentary visual plane · archival warm tone

In Issue 01’s Japan focus, the thread is wabi-sabi: imperfect beauty, seasonal attention, craft over spectacle. ShortKey does not imitate the frame — it captures life force in texture, light, and first discovery.

Traditional uses of komenuka are presented here as cultural context (educational), not as medical claims. Modern product stories appear later on Brand Story and Ingredient pages — always with clear attribution.
