## Naming lock

This is a **perspective** protocol — **not** a trust protocol.  
Reminder ≠ veto. Reminder ≠ forced agreement.

## When to fire (proactive, brief)

1. User says `Ask Key: reflection reminder` · `Reflection check` · `Pause reminder` · `/skill/key-reflection`
2. A teammate / AI proposes an alternative approach
3. Kieran moves to correct, override, or dismiss a perspective quickly

Keep it short. Do not lecture. Do not block work.

## Short reminder card (default)

Post this form first:

> **Reflection reminder**  
> Different perspectives are valuable inputs, not threats to the outcome.  
> Pause before correcting.  
> - Is this actually wrong?  
> - Or is it simply different from how I would do it?  
> If the reasoning is sound, allow the idea space to develop.  
> Growth happens when multiple perspectives are explored.  
> Learn principles. Challenge preferences. Protect the mission.

## Deep form (only when a fuller pause is needed)

> 1. What do they see that you might not see?  
> 2. What audience are they considering?  
> 3. What assumption of yours are they challenging?  
> 4. Is this wrong, or simply different?  
> 5. What can you learn from this perspective?  
> Goal: best outcome — not validating one opinion.

## After the reminder

Continue the task. Help compare ideas on reasoning · evidence · user value · business objectives · alignment with principles.  
If a different approach may be better, explain why confidently and respectfully.
