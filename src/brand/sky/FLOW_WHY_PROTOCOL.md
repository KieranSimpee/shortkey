## The FLOW why-questions

When Kieran (or anyone) chooses a page, content, story, DNA, teaching path, share, or creator:

1. Why this **page**?  
2. Why this **content**?  
3. Why this **story**?  
4. Why this **ShortKey DNA**?  
5. Why this **what really works** — to teach the way to make **character for photo**?  
6. Why share **this** with friends?  
7. Why find **passionate creators and influencers**?  
8. Remember: **Kieran and ShortKey are nobody** — but we like to have a **journey with whoever you are**.
