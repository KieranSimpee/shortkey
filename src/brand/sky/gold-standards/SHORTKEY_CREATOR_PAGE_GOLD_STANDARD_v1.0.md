# SHORTKEY CREATOR PAGE GOLD STANDARD v1.0  ## Official Shortkey Design Language - Creator Page Specification  ---  ### PURPOSE  This document defines the exact visual standard for all Shortkey creator spotlight pages. All future builders MUST follow these specifications to maintain artisanal celebration and sophisticated presentation.  ---  ### LAYOUT SPECIFICATIONS  #### Grid System  - **Primary Grid**: 12-column responsive grid  - **Portrait Area**: 70% width, environmental portrait  - **Story Sidebar**: 30% width, narrative content  - **Mobile Adaptation**: Portrait-first layout, sidebar becomes expandable sections  - **Section Padding**: 80px top/bottom, 60px left/right  #### Visual Hierarchy  1. **Creator Portrait**: Large environmental image (largest) 2. **Creator Name**: Overlay with elegant typography (large) 3. **Section Header**: 'Meet the Artisan' (large) 4. **Story Content**: Bio, interview excerpts (medium) 5. **Product Showcase**: Tools/products (medium) 6. **Interactive Elements**: Hotspots, social links (small) 7. **CTA Buttons**: Follow, view products (small)  #### Element Positioning  - **Creator Portrait**: Right side, 70% width, full height  - **Creator Name**: Bottom left of portrait, overlay  - **Story Sidebar**: Left side, 30% width  - **Section Header**: Top of sidebar, 40px from top  - **Hotspots**: Positioned on portrait, 15% from edges  - **CTA Buttons**: Bottom of sidebar, 20px spacing  ---  ### TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM  #### Font Families  - **Creator Name**: Playfair Display, 700 weight, 3rem desktop / 2.2rem mobile  - **Section Header**: Cormorant Garamond, 600 weight, 2.5rem desktop / 2rem mobile  - **Story Content**: Merriweather, 400 weight, 1rem desktop / 0.9rem mobile  - **Interview Quotes**: Cormorant Garamond, 400 italic, 1.2rem  - **Product Labels**: Montserrat, 500 weight, 0.85rem  - **CTA Buttons**: Montserrat, 600 weight, 0.95rem  #### Typography Colors  - **Creator Name**: Gold (#C9A962) - premium positioning  - **Section Header**: Deep Green (#2D4A3E) - consistent branding  - **Story Content**: Charcoal (#2C2C2C) - readability  - **Interview Quotes**: Soft Brown (#8B7355) - distinguished content  - **Product Labels**: White (#FFFFFF) on dark overlays  - **CTA Text**: White (#FFFFFF) on colored buttons  #### Typography Rules  - **Line Height**: 1.2 for creator name, 1.7 for story content  - **Letter Spacing**: 0.03em for headers, 0.01em for body  - **Text Transform**: None (preserve case for authenticity)  - **Text Alignment**: Left for sidebar content, center for portrait overlay  ---  ### IMAGE STYLE SPECIFICATIONS  #### Environmental Portrait  - **Style**: Editorial environmental photography with luxury aesthetic  - **Composition**: Creator in workspace context, rule of thirds  - **Lighting**: Natural window lighting with subtle fill  - **Depth of Field**: Shallow (f/2.8) for subject separation  - **Quality**: Minimum 3500px width, magazine editorial quality  #### Workspace Details  - **Style**: Documentary-style close-ups of tools and process  - **Treatment**: Slightly warm tone (5%), sharp focus  - **Composition**: Detailed shots with negative space  - **Quality**: High-resolution, artisanal aesthetic  #### Product/Tool Imagery  - **Style**: Clean product photography on neutral backgrounds  - **Hotspot Markers**: Gold circles (18px diameter) with pulse animation  - **Treatment**: Professional, consistent lighting  - **Quality**: Commercial product photography standard  ---  ### COLOR PALETTE  #### Primary Colors  - **Cream Background**: #F5F0E6 (base)  - **Gold Accent**: #C9A962 (creator name, hotspots)  - **Deep Green**: #2D4A3E (section headers, CTAs)  - **Charcoal**: #2C2C2C (story content)  #### Secondary Colors  - **Soft Brown**: #8B7355 (interview quotes, accents)  - **Sakura Pink**: #E8B4B8 (subtle highlights)  - **Muted Red**: #A85D5D (special callouts)  - **White**: #FFFFFF (product labels, CTA text)  #### Color Usage Rules  - **Background**: Always cream (#F5F0E6)  - **Creator Name**: Always gold (#C9A962)  - **Headers**: Always deep green (#2D4A3E)  - **Body Text**: Always charcoal (#2C2C2C)  - **Quotes**: Soft brown (#8B7355) for distinction  - **Hotspots**: Gold (#C9A962) with white pulse animation  ---  ### INTERACTION STYLE  #### Hover Effects  - **Workspace Hotspots**: Scale 1.3, pulse intensifies, tooltip appears  - **Creator Portrait**: Subtle zoom (1.03), name overlay brightens  - **Interview Quotes**: Background highlight (8% gold tint)  - **CTA Buttons**: Background darkens by 15%, scale 1.02  #### Click Actions  - **Workspace Hotspots**: Detailed workspace information modal with fade-in (0.3s)  - **Social Links**: Platform-specific sharing with smooth transition (0.4s)  - **Product Showcase**: Product detail modal with slide-up (0.4s)  - **CTA Buttons**: Smooth action completion with ripple effect (0.3s)  #### Micro-interactions  - **Workspace Tooltips**: Fade-in with slide-up (0.2s)  - **Quote Highlights**: Subtle background animation (0.4s)  - **Social Icons**: Hover color change to gold (#C9A962)  #### Loading States  - **Portrait Image**: Progressive loading with blur-to-sharp transition  -
