# SHORTKEY BRAND STORY PAGE GOLD STANDARD v1.0  ## Official Shortkey Design Language - Brand Story Page Specification  ---  ### PURPOSE  This document defines the exact visual standard for all Shortkey brand story pages. All future builders MUST follow these specifications to maintain artisanal quality and premium brand presentation.  ---  ### LAYOUT SPECIFICATIONS  #### Grid System  - **Primary Grid**: 12-column responsive grid  - **Hero Section**: Full-width hero with overlay text  - **Narrative Flow**: Three-section horizontal scroll (desktop) / vertical stack (mobile)  - **Section Padding**: 100px top/bottom, 80px left/right  #### Visual Hierarchy  1. **Hero Product**: Large visual with brand overlay (largest) 2. **Section Headers**: Heritage, Process, Product (large) 3. **Product Gallery**: Hotspot-enabled product images (medium) 4. **Narrative Text**: Brand story content (medium) 5. **Interactive Elements**: Hotspots, video embeds (small) 6. **Navigation CTAs**: Try products, continue reading (small)  #### Element Positioning  - **Hero Product**: Top center, full-width  - **Brand Logo**: Top left overlay, 40px from edges  - **Section Headers**: Above each narrative section, 60px spacing  - **Product Hotspots**: Positioned on product images, 20% from edges  - **Navigation CTAs**: Bottom right, 40px from content  ---  ### TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM  #### Font Families  - **Brand Name**: Playfair Display, 700 weight, 4rem desktop / 2.8rem mobile  - **Section Headers**: Cormorant Garamond, 600 weight, 2.5rem desktop / 2rem mobile  - **Narrative Text**: Merriweather, 400 weight, 1.1rem desktop / 1rem mobile  - **Product Labels**: Montserrat, 500 weight, 0.9rem  - **CTA Buttons**: Montserrat, 600 weight, 1rem desktop / 0.9rem mobile  #### Typography Colors  - **Brand Name**: Gold (#C9A962) - premium positioning  - **Section Headers**: Deep Green (#2D4A3E) - consistent branding  - **Narrative Text**: Charcoal (#2C2C2C) - readability  - **Product Labels**: White (#FFFFFF) on dark overlays  - **CTA Text**: White (#FFFFFF) on colored buttons  #### Typography Rules  - **Line Height**: 1.2 for brand name, 1.8 for narrative text  - **Letter Spacing**: 0.03em for headers, 0.01em for body  - **Text Transform**: Uppercase for brand name only  - **Text Alignment**: Center for hero elements, left for narrative content  ---  ### IMAGE STYLE SPECIFICATIONS  #### Hero Product Image  - **Style**: Luxury product photography with studio lighting  - **Composition**: Product-centered with elegant negative space  - **Lighting**: Soft, diffused lighting with subtle rim lighting  - **Quality**: Minimum 4000px width, commercial print quality  #### Process Visualization  - **Style**: Clean infographics with gold accent elements  - **Animation**: Subtle motion graphics (0.5s cycles)  - **Treatment**: Minimalist with focus on clarity  - **Quality**: High-resolution vector graphics  #### Product Gallery  - **Style**: Consistent product photography on white/background  - **Hotspot Markers**: Gold circles (20px diameter) with pulse animation  - **Treatment**: Clean, professional, no filters  - **Quality**: E-commerce standard with shadow consistency  ---  ### COLOR PALETTE  #### Primary Colors  - **Cream Background**: #F5F0E6 (base)  - **Gold Accent**: #C9A962 (brand elements, hotspots)  - **Deep Green**: #2D4A3E (section headers, CTAs)  - **Charcoal**: #2C2C2C (narrative text)  #### Secondary Colors  - **Soft Brown**: #8B7355 (decorative elements)  - **Muted Red**: #A85D5D (special callouts)  - **White**: #FFFFFF (product labels, CTA text)  #### Color Usage Rules  - **Background**: Always cream (#F5F0E6)  - **Brand Elements**: Always gold (#C9A962)  - **Headers**: Always deep green (#2D4A3E)  - **Body Text**: Always charcoal (#2C2C2C)  - **Hotspots**: Gold (#C9A962) with white pulse animation  ---  ### INTERACTION STYLE  #### Hover Effects  - **Product Hotspots**: Scale 1.2, pulse intensifies, tooltip appears  - **Section Headers**: Subtle underline animation (0.3s)  - **Product Images**: Subtle zoom (1.05), shadow appears  - **Navigation CTAs**: Background darkens by 15%, scale 1.02  #### Click Actions  - **Product Hotspots**: Detailed product information modal with fade-in (0.3s)  - **Video Embed**: Lightbox modal with smooth transition (0.4s)  - **Process Steps**: Animated progression through transformation story  - **Navigation CTAs**: Smooth page transition (0.5s)  #### Micro-interactions  - **Product Tooltips**: Fade-in with slide-up (0.2s)  - **Process Animations**: Subtle motion (0.5s cycles)  - **Progress Indicators**: Smooth fill animation, 0.6s ease-out  #### Loading States  -
