## EDITORIAL STANDARDS

### Content Pillars
1. **Spectacle**: Visual storytelling and immersive experiences
2. **Education**: Deep-dive cultural and scientific content
3. **Community**: User engagement and participatory features

### Editorial Principles
- **Cultural Respect**: All cultural content must be researched, verified, and presented with appropriate context
- **Scientific Accuracy**: Beauty claims must be supported by research or traditional knowledge attribution
- **Narrative Excellence**: Every piece must tell a compelling story
- **Visual Harmony**: Text and imagery must work in seamless integration
- **Interactive Purpose**: Every interactive element must serve a clear user need

### Country-Specific Frameworks

#### Japan Edition
- **Narrative Thread**: Wabi-sabi (imperfect beauty)
- **Cultural Approach**: Elegant, contemplative, detail-oriented
- **Visual Language**: Minimalist, refined, nature-inspired
- **Key Themes**: Seasonal beauty, ritual, craftsmanship, harmony

#### Korea Edition  
- **Narrative Thread**: Han (suffering) to Hwa (transformation)
- **Cultural Approach**: Dynamic, innovative, resilient
- **Visual Language**: Bold, modern, technology-forward
- **Key Themes**: Transformation, innovation, self-care, fermentation

#### Thailand Edition
- **Narrative Thread**: Sanuk (balance and joy)
- **Cultural Approach**: Warm, welcoming, holistic
- **Visual Language**: Vibrant, natural, spa-inspired
- **Key Themes**: Wellness, herbal wisdom, spirituality, community

### Content Structure Hierarchy
1. **Cover Page**: Hero visual, primary messaging, entry points
2. **Editor's Letter**: Vision statement, issue themes, welcome message
3. **Heritage Story**: Cultural context, historical significance
4. **Ingredient Spotlight**: Scientific and traditional knowledge
5. **Brand Spotlight**: Artisanal stories, product integration
6. **Interactive Experience**: AR, try-on, immersive features
7. **Creator Stories**: Artisanal profiles, behind-the-scenes
8. **Community Engagement**: User-generated content, participation
9. **Seasonal Preview**: Future content teasers

### Editorial Quality Standards
- **Research**: Minimum 3 credible sources per cultural claim
- **Attribution**: Clear sourcing for all traditional knowledge
- **Translation**: Native language terms with phonetic guides
- **Context**: Historical and cultural context always provided
- **Balance**: Traditional wisdom and modern science presented equitably

- **Mobile Adaptation**: Single-column stack with progressive enhancement

### Interactive Design Standards
- **Micro-interactions**: Subtle hover states, smooth transitions
- **Loading States**: Elegant skeleton screens, progress indicators
- **Error States**: Clear, helpful messaging with recovery options
- **Success States**: Celebratory feedback, clear next steps
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance minimum

- **NO traditional knowledge presented without verification**
- **NO cultural elements used solely for aesthetic purposes**
- **ALWAYS consult cultural experts when in doubt**
- **ALWAYS provide historical and contemporary context**
- **ALWAYS respect sacred or ceremonial traditions**

- **NO unverified beauty claims or health benefits**

- **NO misleading or deceptive product representations**
- **NO superficial content without substantive depth**
- **ALWAYS cite sources for scientific claims**
- **ALWAYS distinguish between traditional wisdom and modern science**
- **ALWAYS maintain editorial independence**

- **ALWAYS maintain brand consistency**

### Technical Standards
